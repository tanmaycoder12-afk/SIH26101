# iGOT Karmayogi – Target Users and Purpose

## Who is the iGOT Karmayogi Website Made For?

The **iGOT Karmayogi website** is primarily designed for **government officials and civil servants in India**.

### Main Users

- **Central Government Officials**  
  Officers and employees working in Union Government ministries and departments.

- **State and Union Territory Government Officials**  
  Government employees across different states and Union Territories of India.

- **Government Employees at Different Levels and Roles**  
  The platform supports employees working in different departments, positions and professional roles, including frontline and specialized personnel.

- **Government Administrators, HR and Training Managers**  
  Officials who manage employee training, competencies, learning programmes and capacity-building activities.

## What is iGOT Karmayogi Used For?

iGOT Karmayogi is a **digital learning and professional-development platform for India's civil services**.

Government officials can use the platform to:

- Take training and skill-development courses
- Improve the competencies required for their roles
- Track their learning and training progress
- Earn certificates after completing courses
- Access learning materials and knowledge resources
- Participate in discussions and learning activities
- Attend government-related events and programmes
- Discover relevant learning and professional-development opportunities

## Main Purpose

The main objective of iGOT Karmayogi is to help government employees **continuously improve their knowledge, skills and competencies** so that they can perform their roles more effectively and provide better public services.

## Is iGOT Karmayogi for Ordinary Citizens?

**Not primarily.**

The platform's core audience is **government employees and civil servants**. However, some learning content or course information may be accessible to the general public.

## In One Sentence

> **iGOT Karmayogi is a digital learning and competency-development platform primarily designed for people working in the Indian Government to improve their knowledge, skills, competencies and effectiveness in public service.**
